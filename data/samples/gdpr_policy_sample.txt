ACME CORPORATION — DATA PROTECTION POLICY
Version 2.1 | Last Updated: January 2026
Classification: Internal

─────────────────────────────────────────
1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
─────────────────────────────────────────
This policy sets out how Acme Corporation ("the Company") collects, uses, stores, and
protects personal data in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (EU)
2016/679 ("GDPR") and applicable national data protection legislation.

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third-party processors who handle
personal data on behalf of the Company.

─────────────────────────────────────────
2. DATA PROTECTION PRINCIPLES (Art. 5)
─────────────────────────────────────────
The Company processes personal data in accordance with the following principles:

2.1 Lawfulness, Fairness and Transparency
Personal data is processed lawfully. Data subjects are informed of processing activities
via privacy notices published on the Company website and provided at point of collection.

2.2 Purpose Limitation
Personal data is collected for specified, explicit and legitimate purposes. We collect
employee data for HR administration, payroll, and performance management. Customer data
is collected for contract fulfilment and account management.

2.3 Data Minimisation
We collect only the data necessary for the stated purpose. Data collection forms are
reviewed annually to remove unnecessary fields.

2.4 Accuracy
We maintain procedures to ensure data accuracy. Employees may request corrections to
their HR records through the HR portal.

2.5 Storage Limitation
Customer account data is retained for the duration of the contract plus 3 years for
legal purposes. Employee payroll records are retained for 7 years per statutory
requirements. Marketing contact lists are reviewed annually.

NOTE: The Company does not currently maintain a formal data retention schedule mapping
each data category to a defined retention period. This represents a gap in our Art. 5(1)(e)
compliance posture.

2.6 Integrity and Confidentiality
Personal data is protected by appropriate technical and organisational measures including
encryption at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3), access controls, and regular
security assessments.

─────────────────────────────────────────
3. LAWFUL BASIS FOR PROCESSING (Art. 6)
─────────────────────────────────────────
The Company relies on the following lawful bases:

- Contract performance: processing employee and customer data necessary to fulfil
  contractual obligations
- Legal obligation: payroll, tax, and statutory reporting requirements
- Legitimate interests: fraud prevention, network security, business analytics
- Consent: marketing communications (opt-in required, withdrawal mechanism provided)

─────────────────────────────────────────
4. DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS
─────────────────────────────────────────
4.1 Right of Access (Art. 15)
Data subjects may submit Subject Access Requests (SARs) via email to dpo@acme.com.
Requests are acknowledged within 3 business days and fulfilled within 30 days.
The DPO maintains a SAR log tracking all requests and responses.

4.2 Right to Rectification (Art. 16)
Data subjects may request correction of inaccurate data. Correction requests are
processed within 10 business days.

4.3 Right to Erasure (Art. 17)
The Company recognises data subjects' right to request deletion of their personal data.
Requests should be submitted to dpo@acme.com.

*** COMPLIANCE GAP: The Company does not currently have a documented procedure for
handling Art. 17 erasure requests, including: verification steps, propagation to
downstream processors, timelines for completion, or logging of erasure actions.
This must be remediated as a priority. ***

4.4 Right to Data Portability (Art. 20)
Customer data export is available in CSV format via the customer portal. Employee data
portability requests are handled by HR on a case-by-case basis.

─────────────────────────────────────────
6. DATA SECURITY (Art. 32)
─────────────────────────────────────────
6.1 Technical Measures
- Encryption at rest: AES-256 for all databases containing personal data
- Encryption in transit: TLS 1.3 enforced for all API endpoints
- Access controls: Role-based access control (RBAC) with principle of least privilege
- Multi-factor authentication: Required for all staff accessing personal data systems
- Vulnerability scanning: Monthly automated scans plus annual penetration test

6.2 Organisational Measures
- Staff training: Annual GDPR awareness training (completion rate: 94% last cycle)
- Background checks: Conducted for all staff with access to sensitive personal data
- Supplier due diligence: Data processing agreements in place with all major processors

─────────────────────────────────────────
7. DATA BREACH NOTIFICATION (Art. 33/34)
─────────────────────────────────────────
The Company maintains an Incident Response Plan covering personal data breaches.
Suspected breaches must be reported to the DPO within 24 hours of discovery.
The DPO will assess whether notification to the supervisory authority is required
within the 72-hour window per Art. 33.

9. INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS
─────────────────────────────────────────
Data transfers to third countries are made under Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)
approved by the European Commission. A transfer impact assessment (TIA) is conducted
for each new transfer to countries without an adequacy decision.
