SOX - Sarbanes-Oxley Act of 2002
Key Sections for Compliance Assessment:

Section 302 - Corporate responsibility for financial reports
  Principal executive and financial officers must certify that quarterly and annual reports do not contain
  untrue statements, fairly present the financial condition and results of operations, and that they are
  responsible for establishing and maintaining disclosure controls and procedures.

Section 404 - Management assessment of internal controls
  Annual reports must contain an internal control report that:
  - States the responsibility of management for establishing and maintaining adequate internal controls;
  - Identifies the framework used to evaluate effectiveness;
  - Contains management's assessment of the effectiveness of internal controls over financial reporting;
  - The registered public accounting firm must attest to and report on management's assessment.

Section 409 - Real time disclosure
  Companies must disclose to the public on a rapid and current basis material changes in financial condition
  or operations. Disclosures shall be presented in terms that are easy to understand.

Section 802 - Criminal penalties for altering documents
  Whoever knowingly alters, destroys, mutilates, conceals, covers up, falsifies, or makes a false entry in
  any record, document, or tangible object with the intent to impede, obstruct, or influence the investigation
  shall be fined or imprisoned.

IT Controls under SOX:
  - Access controls: restrict access to financial systems to authorised personnel;
  - Change management: document and approve all changes to financial systems;
  - Data backup and recovery: maintain backups with tested recovery procedures;
  - Audit trails: log all access to and changes in financial data with timestamps;
  - Segregation of duties: no single person should have end-to-end control of a financial process.
